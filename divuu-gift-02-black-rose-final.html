gift 02 · for Divuuu, who wished for a black rose

For Divuuu one black rose, finally

A flower that is difficult to find here, but somehow became one of the little wishes you wanted someone to fulfil with their own hands.

tap the rose 🖤🌹

“Kaash koi mujhe black rose de…”

Divuuu bacha, aapne kabhi black rose ke baare mein bataya tha… aur tab se mere dimaag mein ek chhoti si baat reh gayi.

Aapko black rose itna pasand hai, aur aapka wish hai ki koi aapko woh apne haath se de. Aur mujhe sabse zyada bura isi baat ka laga ki main screen ke is paar hoon, aapke paas nahi… warna shayad main bas ek black rose lekar aapke saamne khada hota aur bina kuch clever bole aapko de deta.

Aur Amazon jungle wala woh doosra beautiful flower bhi yaad hai jo aapko pasand hai. Kuch cheezein chhoti lagti hain, lekin jab kisi apne ki favourite hoti hain na… toh woh chhoti nahi rehti.

“Aapko phool dene ki wish thi…
isliye maine ek chhota sa garden bana diya.” 🖤

Main jaanta hoon ye screen par bana hua rose hai. Isme mitti ki khushboo nahi hai, iske kaante nahi hain, aur ye aapke haath mein pakda hua real flower bhi nahi hai.

But I wanted to make one thing real — the thought behind it.

Agar kabhi koi aapko black rose na de paaye, toh please ye mat sochna ki aapka wish chhota tha. Aapke little wishes matter. Aapki favourite cheezein matter. Aapki woh random baatein jo aap casually bol deti ho… woh bhi mere liye yaad rakhne layak hain.

And one day, when distance isn't sitting between us, I still want to give you a real black rose with my own hands.

Tab shayad main kuch bol bhi na paun, kyunki aapke saamne aakar main waise bhi shy ho jaata hoon. 😂

Bas flower aapko de dunga… aur shayad dheere se bolunga:

“Ye woh wala hai na, jo aap chahti thi?” 🌹

Is rose ka colour black hai… but meri wish aapke liye bilkul opposite hai.

Main chahta hoon aapki life mein jitne bhi dark days aaye hain, unke baad aapko hamesha thoda sa light mile.

Jab aap lonely feel karo — yaad rakhna, aapko samajhne ki koshish karne wala ek insaan hai.

Jab aapko lage ki koi aapko nahi samajhta — please khud ko prove karne mein mat thakna.

Jab aap sad ho — moon ko dekh lena. 🌙

And when you see this black rose… remember that somewhere, a boy who can't draw flowers properly still tried to make one for his favourite girl.

“Real black rose abhi nahi de paaya…
par uski wish yaad rakhi hai, Divuuu bacha.” 🖤🌹

— aapka Shubham ❤️
